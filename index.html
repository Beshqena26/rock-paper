Rock Paper Scissors is a multiplier climbing game. Compete against the house in classic RPS rounds — each win increases your payout. Cash out anytime or risk it all.

How to Play

  • Place a bet — enter your amount and click Place Bet.
  • Pick your hand — choose Rock ✊, Paper ✋, or Scissors ✌️.
  • Win — multiplier increases. Cash out or keep going.
  • Tie — moves to next card, same multiplier. Ties are free.
  • Lose — you lose your bet. Game over.
  • Cash out — collect winnings anytime after first win.
  • Cancel — before first pick, cancel to get your bet back.

Rules

  • Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
  • Each win compounds the multiplier up to 100x.
  • Ties don't count — extra cards are added dynamically.
  • 7 wins = Maxed Out (100x). Must win 1 round to cash out.

House Edge & RTP

📊

RTP: 96.5% — House edge is 3.5% per round. Base multiplier is 1.93x. 7 wins reaches 100x. Max bet $100, max win $10,000.

Tips

  • Every outcome is provably fair — click Fair Play to verify.
  • Each round is independent — previous results don't affect future ones.
  • Higher multipliers = higher risk. Cash out when comfortable.
  • Ties are free — more gameplay at no cost.

How it works
  1. Server generates a seed and shows you its SHA256 hash
  2. You set your client seed (your influence on randomness)
  3. You place a bet
  4. Roll = HMAC-SHA256(server_seed, your_seed:0)
  5. Server reveals the seed — verify SHA256 matches
  6. New seed pre-generated for next bet
