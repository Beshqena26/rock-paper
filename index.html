Rules

Rock Paper Scissors is a simple and exciting game where players choose rock, paper, or scissors in competition against the house to come out on top and increase their multiplier streak.

  • Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, and paper beats rock.
  • Each win increases the player's multiplier, building up to a maximum of 10,000x.
  • In manual mode, ties move the game forward to the next stage without affecting the multiplier.
  • In auto mode, ties pay 0.98x.
  • To cash out in manual mode, the player must win at least one round.
  • There are 10 stages in total. Clearing all stages awards the final multiplier.
  • Players can cash out at any point after winning at least one round.

Fair Play

This game uses a provably fair system to guarantee that neither the player nor the house can manipulate the outcome of any round.

  • Server Seed: A cryptographic seed is generated server-side before each game begins. A SHA-256 hash of this seed is shown to the player before play starts.
  • Client Seed: The player provides their own seed (or one is generated automatically), which is combined with the server seed to determine outcomes.
  • Verification: After each game, the unhashed server seed is revealed so players can independently verify that the outcomes were not altered.
  • House Edge: 2% per round. The base win multiplier is 1.96x (2x × 0.98), compounding each stage.
  • RNG: Each round's house choice is derived from HMAC-SHA256(serverSeed, clientSeed + nonce + round), ensuring true randomness that neither party can predict.
